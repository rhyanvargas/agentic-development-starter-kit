# ADR template

Load when recording a major irreversible architecture choice.

```markdown
# ADR-[number]: [decision title]

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem, constraint, scale, or risk requires a decision?

## Decision

What option was selected and where does it apply?

## Alternatives

What realistic options were considered?

## Benefits

What measurable advantages does the selected option provide?

## Trade-offs

What cost, complexity, coupling, latency, or limitation is accepted?

## Risks and mitigations

What can go wrong, and which controls reduce the risk?

## Consequences

What new responsibilities, dependencies, tests, and operational work result?
```

Filename suggestion: `docs/architecture/adrs/ADR-NNN-short-title.md` (or project convention).
